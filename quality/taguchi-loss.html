Quality Management · Taguchi

Taguchi Loss Function

The traditional "goalpost" view says a unit inside the specification limits costs nothing, and a unit outside costs the full price of a defect. Taguchi says every gram away from the target costs something, and the cost grows with the square of the distance. Compare both views on 400 g salmon fillet packs (limits 390 to 410 g), and on the classic Sony TV story.

Key ideas

Goalpost thinking

Inside the limits is "good", outside is "bad". A pack at 409.9 g and one at 400.0 g are treated as equally good, while 410.1 g is suddenly a full defect.

Taguchi's quadratic loss

L(y) = k (y − T)², k = C / Δ²

C is the cost at the limit, Δ the distance from target to limit (10 g). Loss starts at the first gram off target.

Expected loss per unit

E[L] = k [σ² + (μ − T)²]

Two levers: reduce the spread (σ) and put the mean on target (μ = T). Being inside the limits is not enough.

Sony, 1979

The Japanese plant made colour density as a bell curve centred on target, with a few sets out of spec. The San Diego plant had no sets out of spec, but spread them evenly across the range. Customers preferred the Japanese sets.

Check your understanding

1. Load the two Sony scenarios. Which plant has fewer defects, and which has the lower Taguchi loss? Why?

San Diego has 0 % out of spec, Japan 0.27 %. But San Diego's flat spread has σ = 5.77 g against 3.33 g in Japan, so its expected loss is three times higher (NOK 6.67 against 2.22 per unit with C = NOK 20). Many San Diego sets sit near the limits, where they are "in spec" but close to being bad.

2. With C = NOK 20 and Δ = 10 g, what is k, and what is the loss for one pack weighing 405 g?

k = 20 / 10² = 0.2 NOK per g². L(405) = 0.2 × 5² = NOK 5. The goalpost view says NOK 0.

3. Load "Nordfjord today" (μ = 402 g, σ = 2.5 g). How much of the loss comes from running 2 g above target?

Off-target part: 0.2 × 2² = NOK 0.80 per pack, about NOK 480,000 a year at 600,000 packs. Spread part: 0.2 × 2.5² = NOK 1.25. Re-centring at 400 g removes the 0.80 at no cost, without touching the machine's spread.

4. Compare packs at 409.9 g and 410.1 g in both views.

Goalpost: NOK 0 and NOK 20, a jump at the line. Taguchi: about NOK 19.6 and NOK 20.4, almost the same, because the customer can hardly tell them apart.
