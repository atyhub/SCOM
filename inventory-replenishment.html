When should we reorder?

Stock is used at a steady usage rate d. When the inventory position falls to the reorder point (ROP), an order of Q units is placed. It arrives one lead time L later, just as stock runs out. Press play and watch the sawtooth build.

With constant demand, the order arrives exactly when stock hits zero. Add variation and some lead times end in a stockout (red, shown as backorders). Then raise the safety stock and watch the stockouts disappear: the ROP rises to d × L + SS and the whole sawtooth lifts by SS.

The trigger is the inventory position (on hand + on order), not on-hand stock. Set L longer than Q/d to see why: more than one order is in transit at once.
